# Gradient Descent

Gradient descent treats optimization as movement across a loss surface. Each step follows the negative gradient, and the learning rate decides whether the path is slow, stable, or unstable. Parameter fitting becomes visible as a trajectory.

## Open

- [gradient-descent.ipynb](gradient-descent.ipynb)

## What To Watch

- The gradient points toward steepest local increase.
- A negative-gradient step attempts to lower loss.
- Large learning rates can overshoot or diverge.
- Convex losses have simpler guarantees than non-convex losses.
